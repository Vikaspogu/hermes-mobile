verified Material 3 Expressive Architecture Notes

  • 1. Expressive Shape Grammar & Connected Lists Uses Android 16 M3 Expressive mixed corner radii: 28px outer caps paired with 8px inner seams visually bind Today/Yesterday thread groups and multi-step tool call sequences into cohesive physical stacks, while 12-lobe scalloped cookie badges distinguish live AI entities.
  • 2. Viridian + Kinetic Persimmon Tonal Hierarchy Avoids generic blue by pairing a calm Botanical Viridian seed (#006B5B) for steady operational surfaces with a high-energy Kinetic Persimmon tertiary accent (#A73912 / #FFB59C) reserved strictly for voice capture, new-thread initiation, and risk callouts.
  • 3. One-Handed Thumb-Zone Ergonomics Replaces edge-to-edge bottom chrome with a floating 66px capsule NavigationBar and companion squircle FAB anchored in the natural thumb arc. Every interactive control (filter chips, mic, slide-to-approve, deny/approve buttons) enforces a ≥48×48px touch target.
  • 4. Deliberate Friction for High-Impact Approvals Low/medium-risk actions use 48px single-tap buttons, while destructive commands (rm -rf ./build) require a 48px tactile Slide-to-Approve gesture track next to an isolated Deny button—preventing accidental pocket or fat-finger execution.
  • 5. Trade-offs vs. Dense Utility Expressive typography (Bricolage Grotesque headers), 28px group caps, and floating toolbars trade ~10% vertical row density for dramatically faster visual scanning, unmistakable Android native personality, and zero mis-taps during on-the-go cluster triage.
